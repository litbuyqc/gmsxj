What is a Joyagoo spreadsheet?

A Joyagoo spreadsheet is a product research list organized around shopping with Joyagoo. It can record links, categories, prices, sizes and comparison notes. This page provides an independent discovery guide and CAFANS category links; it is not an official Joyagoo sheet or a live inventory feed.

02 / Joyagoo shopping guide

Build a shortlist
you can actually use.

A useful Joyagoo product list should help you make a decision. Keep the original listing, your preferred variant and the reasons an item fits your needs together.

For clothing, prioritize measurements and fabric information. For accessories, record dimensions and closure details. Leave uncertain fields marked as unverified.

Choose one category first

Start with shoes, hoodies, T-shirts or another clear category. A focused search makes similar items easier to compare.

Save the exact product and variant

Record the listing URL, seller, selected color, size and date checked. A general photograph does not identify the exact version being sold.

Check fit and supporting evidence

Compare measurements with an item you own. Review listing photographs, material details and return terms; ask for clarification when information is missing.

Estimate the complete cost

Include the item price, applicable service fees, delivery costs, currency conversion and possible import charges. Confirm requirements with your chosen shopping service before payment.

04 / Clear answers

Before you

Quick answers about using a Joyagoo spreadsheet, comparing products and understanding the limits of a discovery guide.

What is a Joyagoo spreadsheet?

A Joyagoo spreadsheet is a product research list organized around shopping with Joyagoo. It can record product links, categories, prices, sizes and comparison notes. This page is an independent category guide, not an official Joyagoo spreadsheet or a live inventory feed.

How do I use this guide?

Choose a category to open CAFANS, review the available product information and save relevant items to your own shortlist. Confirm purchase requirements and link compatibility with your chosen shopping service before ordering.

Does a spreadsheet prove product authenticity or quality?

No. A spreadsheet entry, brand name or reference photograph does not establish authenticity, seller authorization or product quality. Check seller evidence, product details and return terms independently.

How should I compare clothing and sneaker sizes?

Compare garment measurements with clothing you already own. For sneakers, compare the seller’s size chart and foot-length guidance rather than relying only on a regional size label.

Are prices and shipping costs guaranteed?

No. This guide does not quote live prices or shipping rates. Confirm the item price, service fees, delivery costs, currency conversion and any applicable import charges before payment.

Is this website affiliated with Joyagoo or the featured brands?

No. This is an independent discovery guide that directs visitors to CAFANS. Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Hermès and Joyagoo are referenced for identification and editorial context; no affiliation or endorsement is claimed.
